隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

中文

小说聚合平台（Novel Hub）尊重并保护您的隐私。本政策说明本应用如何处理信息。

1. 我们收集的信息 / Information We Collect

中文

本应用不收集、不上传任何个人身份信息。您创作的全部小说、章节、大纲与阅读进度均仅保存在您的设备本地。

English

This app does not collect or upload any personally identifiable information. All novels, chapters, outlines, and reading progress you create are stored only on your device.

2. 广告 / Advertising

中文

本应用接入穿山甲(Pangle)聚合广告SDK，用于展示广告以维持免费服务。本应用内会展示由第三方广告平台提供的广告，包括开屏、横幅（Banner）、插屏与信息流广告。

为完成广告的加载、展示频次控制、反作弊与效果归因，广告SDK可能会收集：设备标识(IDFA/OAID)、设备型号、操作系统版本、网络类型、粗略位置(非精确定位)等非个人设备信息。按照 Apple 要求，应用会通过「App 跟踪透明度（ATT）」弹窗征得知情同意：只有在您明确授权后，广告 SDK 才会访问广告标识符用于跨应用跟踪；若您拒绝，则仅使用不可关联到个人的基础设备信息用于广告投放，不会进行跨应用跟踪。您也可随时在系统「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪」中关闭广告个性化。

本应用开发者自身不收集、不出售、亦不共享任何个人信息。广告相关的数据处理由上述第三方广告平台在其各自的隐私政策与 ATT 授权范围内独立完成，我们不会将您的账号、姓名、联系方式等个人身份信息提供给广告平台用于定向投放。

English

This app integrates the Pangle ad SDK and displays advertisements to keep the service free. It shows ads served by third-party advertising platforms, including splash (launch) ads, banner ads, interstitial ads, and native (in-feed) ads.

To perform ad loading, frequency capping, anti-fraud, and attribution, the relevant ad SDKs may access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA/OAID) along with non-personal device information such as device model, OS version, network type, and coarse (non-precise) location. In accordance with Apple's requirements, the app requests consent through the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt: only after your explicit authorization will the ad SDK access the advertising identifier for cross-app tracking; if you decline, only non-personally-identifiable basic device information is used for ad delivery, and no cross-app tracking occurs. You can turn off ad personalization at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

The app developer itself does not collect, sell, or share any personal information. Ad-related data processing is performed independently by the aforementioned third-party advertising platforms under their own privacy policies and within the scope of ATT authorization. We do not provide your account, name, contact details, or other personal identifiers to advertisers for targeted delivery.

3. AI 生成与网络请求 / AI Generation & Network Requests

中文

当您主动点击「开始创作」或「续写下一章」时，应用会将书名、题材、简介、世界观/人物及已有章节摘要发送至您自行配置的 AI 服务接口（如 DeepSeek 或其他 OpenAI 兼容接口）以生成内容。该请求不包含任何个人身份信息。除此之外，应用不会在后台上传您的任何数据。

English

When you tap "Start Creating" or "Continue Next Chapter", the app sends the title, genre, synopsis, world setting, and a summary of existing chapters to the AI endpoint you configured yourself (e.g. DeepSeek or any OpenAI-compatible API) to generate content. No personal information is included. Apart from that, the app does not upload any of your data in the background.

4. API Key 存储 / API Key Storage

中文

您填写的 API Key 仅保存在本机（本机偏好设置），不会上传至我们的服务器（我们没有任何服务器）。您可以随时在「设置」中修改或删除。删除本应用会一并清除该 Key。

English

Your API Key is stored only on your device (local preferences) and is never uploaded to our servers (we have none). You may edit or remove it at any time in Settings. Deleting the app removes the key.

5. 第三方服务 / Third-Party Services

中文

AI 生成功能使用您自行配置的第三方接口，其数据处理受该服务商自身的隐私政策约束。您可以完全不使用此功能，不影响本地已生成内容的阅读与导出。

English

The AI generation feature uses a third-party endpoint you configure yourself; its data handling is governed by that provider's own privacy policy. You may choose not to use this feature; it does not affect reading or exporting content already generated locally.

6. 内容版权 / Content Ownership

中文

您通过本应用创作的全部内容归您所有。应用不主张任何权利，也不对外分发您的作品，除非您主动使用「导出 / 分享」功能。

English

All content you create with this app belongs to you. The app claims no rights and does not distribute your works, unless you actively use the "Export / Share" feature.
