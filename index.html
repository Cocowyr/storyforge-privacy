隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

中文

小说聚合平台（Novel Hub）尊重并保护您的隐私。本政策说明本应用如何处理信息。

1. 我们收集的信息 / Information We Collect

中文

本应用不收集、不上传任何个人身份信息。您创作的全部小说、章节、大纲与阅读进度均仅保存在您的设备本地。

English

This app does not collect or upload any personally identifiable information. All novels, chapters, outlines, and reading progress you create are stored only on your device.

2. AI 生成与网络请求 / AI Generation & Network Requests

中文

当您主动点击「开始创作」或「续写下一章」时，应用会将书名、题材、简介、世界观/人物及已有章节摘要发送至您自行配置的 AI 服务接口（如 DeepSeek 或其他 OpenAI 兼容接口）以生成内容。该请求不包含任何个人身份信息。除此之外，应用不会在后台上传您的任何数据。

English

When you tap "Start Creating" or "Continue Next Chapter", the app sends the title, genre, synopsis, world setting, and a summary of existing chapters to the AI endpoint you configured yourself (e.g. DeepSeek or any OpenAI-compatible API) to generate content. No personal information is included. Apart from that, the app does not upload any of your data in the background.

3. API Key 存储 / API Key Storage

中文

您填写的 API Key 仅保存在本机（本机偏好设置），不会上传至我们的服务器（我们没有任何服务器）。您可以随时在「设置」中修改或删除。删除本应用会一并清除该 Key。

English

Your API Key is stored only on your device (local preferences) and is never uploaded to our servers (we have none). You may edit or remove it at any time in Settings. Deleting the app removes the key.

4. 第三方服务 / Third-Party Services

中文

AI 生成功能使用您自行配置的第三方接口，其数据处理受该服务商自身的隐私政策约束。您可以完全不使用此功能，不影响本地已生成内容的阅读与导出。

English

The AI generation feature uses a third-party endpoint you configure yourself; its data handling is governed by that provider's own privacy policy. You may choose not to use this feature; it does not affect reading or exporting content already generated locally.

5. 内容版权 / Content Ownership

中文

您通过本应用创作的全部内容归您所有。应用不主张任何权利，也不对外分发您的作品，除非您主动使用「导出 / 分享」功能。

English

All content you create with this app belongs to you. The app claims no rights and does not distribute your works, unless you actively use the "Export / Share" feature.
